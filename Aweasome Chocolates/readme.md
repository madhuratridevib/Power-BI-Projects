# Awesome Chocolates – Sales & Revenue Analysis - Power BI Project
## 📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales and revenue data of Awesome Chocolates to identify performance trends, product profitability, and regional sales distribution. The dashboard helps in understanding revenue growth patterns and business performance metrics.

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- MS Excel

## 📌 Key Analysis & Insights
- Identified top-performing products based on revenue and profit margin.
- Analyzed monthly and yearly sales growth trends.
- Compared regional sales performance.
- Detected underperforming product categories.
- Designed KPI metrics for business decision-making.
